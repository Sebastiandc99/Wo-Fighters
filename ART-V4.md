Full objects entirely contained within each cell, 8% transparent padding all sides, never touch neighboring objects. Yellow counterweight at rear LEFT. No driver. Top-right: large blue 20-foot shipping CONTAINER, side view with shallow visible right end, corrugated steel walls, corner castings, right end double doors with four silver vertical locking bars, thick navy outlined corners, bright highlights. A real cargo container, not an abstract rectangle. Bottom-left: a heavy CRANE HOOK assembly: yellow-and-black striped pulley block, visible circular pulley, huge polished curved steel J-hook hanging from swivel below; full isolated assembly, no rope extending to cell edge. Bottom-right: a load of three long steel I-beams strapped as a bundle, broad steel top flanges and cut ends clearly visible, yellow lifting slings forming an inverted V above with attachment ring. Crisp bold dark outlines and readable silhouettes at small game sizes, rich 16-bit pixel shading matching classic arcade fighters. Industrial yellow/blue/steel palette. NO text, letters, company logos, brand names, scenery, floor, glow, drop shadow or labels. Output square 2048x2048 transparent PNG.
